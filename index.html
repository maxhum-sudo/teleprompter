Support

Teleprompter is an iPhone app that puts your script on the screen, next to the front camera, so you can look into the lens while you record. The saved video is just you.

How to use the app Email support

Something isn’t working

Camera access is off

Recording needs the front camera. On the prompter screen, tap Open Settings, then turn Camera on for Teleprompter. You can also open Settings → Teleprompter → Camera.

The video has no sound

The app records your voice with the video. If the microphone is off, the prompter says the video will be silent. Turn Microphone on in Settings → Teleprompter, then record again.

Save to Photos failed

Saving needs permission to add videos to Photos. If you see “Photos access is off,” open Settings → Teleprompter → Photos and allow access, then tap Try again. You can also tap Share and send the video somewhere else.

There is no camera picture

Teleprompter uses the front camera. On a device without one, you can still read the script, but you cannot record.

Start is dimmed

Write at least a few words in the script. Start stays off while the script is empty.
