Privacy

Teleprompter does not have an account, and it does not send your script or your videos to a server. This policy was last updated on November 19, 2026.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your script, text size, and scroll speed are stored on the device.
  • A recording exists in the app until you save it, share it, or leave it behind.
  • Nothing in the app is uploaded by Teleprompter. There is no analytics and no advertising.

Camera

The front camera shows you the picture you are recording and captures the video. Teleprompter uses it so you can read the script while looking toward the lens. The words are drawn on the screen only. They are not part of the saved video.

Microphone

The microphone records your voice into the video. If you decline microphone access, you can still record a silent video.

Photos

Photos access is used only when you tap Save to Photos, and only to add that video to your library. Teleprompter does not read the rest of your photo library.

Sharing

If you tap Share, iOS hands the video to the app you pick. What that app does with the file is covered by its own policy.
