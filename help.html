Help

Write the words, press Start, and read them over the front camera. The video that saves is only you. The words stay on the screen.

Write the script

  1. Open Teleprompter. The editor is the first screen.
  2. Type or paste your script. It stays on this iPhone.
  3. Tap Done on the keyboard when you are finished editing.
  4. Tap Start. Start stays off until the script has some text.

Read on camera

The front camera fills the screen and the script sits near the top, close to the lens. Allow Camera and Microphone the first time you are asked.

  • Speed changes how fast the words move. Size changes how large they are. Both sliders are available before you record.
  • Play starts the scroll. Tap it again to pause.
  • Drag the script up or down to jump ahead or go back. Dragging pauses the scroll.
  • The curved arrow sends the script back to the start.

Record

  1. Tap the red record button.
  2. A countdown of 3, 2, 1 gives you a moment to settle. Tap the record button or the close button during the countdown to cancel it.
  3. After the countdown, recording starts and the script scrolls.
  4. A red timer shows how long you have been recording.
  5. Tap the square in the record button, or the close button, to stop.

Speed and size stay fixed while the countdown or the recording is running. If the microphone is off, a note says the video will be silent.

Save or share

When you stop, the recording plays back.

  • Save to Photos adds the video to your library. The button changes to Saved when it finishes.
  • Share opens the iPhone share sheet so you can send the video another way.
  • Record again returns to the prompter with the script at the start.
  • The close button leaves the recording and returns to the editor. Save or share first if you want to keep that take.

Leave the prompter

Tap the close button when you are not recording to return to the editor. Your script, size, and speed are kept for the next time you open the app.
